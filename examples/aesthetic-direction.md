## Thesis and DNA

Proposed Visual Thesis: “An ivory-paper AI portfolio where oversized left-aligned grotesk type yields to one dark, wide workflow workspace, tiny technical labels establish a secondary voice, and a quiet right margin gives the opening screen a deliberate imbalance.”

First focus: headline; second: real workflow canvas; third: contact action. Dominant visual: headline at entry, workspace at first transition. Silhouette: left-heavy, layered, not centered. Scale: large display against compact metadata, readable body text. Typography: broad grotesk display, neutral prose, restrained mono labels. Negative space: broad margin in hero, tighter project index. Depth: paper background → workspace plane → one intentionally inset annotation. Imagery: real project UI, not a stock orb. Emotional temperature: thoughtful technical workshop. Signature: the title and workflow meet at a single cropped seam.

## Art Direction

- Composition: let the headline claim the opening; permit the workspace to enter the first transition; retain at most two major competing anchors.
- Typography: decide wraps with real text and actual fonts; compact metadata provides contrast without making essential text unreadable. Check CJK and fallback widths if the site is Chinese.
- Imagery: capture a real project with its prototype status visible; use a focal crop that still explains the task. Until captured, imagery is provisional.
- Color/depth: ivory, near-black, one contextual accent. Depth comes from overlap and framing rather than glass on every control.
- Motion: one short state change tied to task execution; keep the static signature intact and honor reduced motion.
- Anti-Patterns: equal glowing cards as hero; repeated centered three-column grids; unreadable miniature UI; fabricated performance metrics. Replace these with a dominant workspace and a clear project index.
